## 📸 PHOTO STRATEGY

### **Types of Photos That Work:**

**1. Project Photos (Best):**
- Completed roofing projects
- Before/after comparisons
- Work in progress
- Different roofing materials (tuiles, zinc, ardoise)

**2. Team Photos:**
- You on a roof (safety gear)
- Team with branded vehicle
- Professional headshots

**3. Equipment Photos:**
- Branded trucks/vans
- Tools and materials
- Safety equipment

### **Photo Tips:**
- **Good lighting** (natural light, no shadows)
- **High quality** (clear, not blurry)
- **Show branding** (logos on trucks/vests)
- **Include people** (human connection)
- **Variety** (don't post same photo twice)

---

## 🎯 POSTING BEST PRACTICES

### **DO:**
✅ Post 2-3x per week consistently
✅ Respond to comments/messages within 1 hour
✅ Use clear, high-quality photos
✅ Include phone number in every post
✅ Add location (Toulon, Var 83)
✅ Use relevant hashtags (#couverture #toiture #toulon)
✅ Respond to ALL messages (even if just "Merci")

### **DON'T:**
❌ Post less than 1x per week (not enough visibility)
❌ Use low-quality or blurry photos
❌ Forget phone number (can't contact you!)
❌ Ignore messages (looks unprofessional)
❌ Post the same content repeatedly
❌ Over-post (3+ times per day = spammy)
❌ Use too many hashtags (5-7 is enough)

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### **Maximize Reach:**

1. **Post at optimal times:**
   - **Monday-Friday:** 8am-9am, 12pm-1pm, 6pm-8pm
   - **Saturday:** 9am-11am, 2pm-4pm
   - **Sunday:** Avoid (low engagement)

2. **Boost top posts:**
   - If a post gets 100+ views, boost it €5-10
   - Targets more people in your area
   - Usually gets 2-3x more reach

3. **Engage with others:**
   - Like/comment on other local business posts
   - Participate in local groups (not just posting)
   - Build relationships = more referrals

4. **Use video (optional but powerful):**
   - 30-second video of you working
   - Time-lapse of project completion
   - Before/after video walkthrough
   - Videos get 2-3x more engagement than photos
